Common questions

How do I save my farm? Your farm saves automatically on your device as you play.

Why am I seeing ads? Sunny Farm Valley is free and supported by ads. Rewarded ads are always optional — you choose when to watch one. Occasional interstitial ads may appear at level-ups.

How do I stop personalized ads? When the app asks about tracking, choose "Ask App Not to Track", or change it any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
