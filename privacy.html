Sunny Farm Valley — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Sunny Farm Valley is developed by Adrian Hinz. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app itself collects

The app does not require an account and has no sign-in. Your gameplay and farm progress are stored only on your device. The developer operates no server that receives your gameplay data.

Advertising data

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect the following data types:

  • Device ID (for example, the device advertising identifier)
  • Product Interaction (how the app and its ads are used)
  • Advertising Data (information about the ads shown and interacted with)

These data types are used for third-party advertising and may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, as described in Google's policies. They are not linked to your identity by the developer. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track" in the iOS tracking prompt, personalized advertising is turned off.

Your choices

You can change tracking permissions any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Deleting the app removes all locally stored gameplay data.
